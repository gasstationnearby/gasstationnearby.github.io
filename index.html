Frequently asked questions

Why do gas prices vary so much between stations?

Taxes, rent, wholesale costs, and competition all play a role. Stations near highways and in expensive neighborhoods usually charge more, while stations with heavy local competition tend to price lower. A station a few blocks away can easily be 20–40 cents cheaper per gallon.

Is premium gas worth the extra money?

Only if your car actually requires it. Most cars are designed for regular 87-octane fuel, and using premium in them gives no benefit. Check your owner's manual: if it says 'premium required', use it; if it says 'premium recommended', regular is usually fine, with only a small performance difference.

How accurate are these calculators?

They are as accurate as the numbers you enter. Use your real average MPG (from your dashboard or a fuel log) and the current pump price for the best estimate. Real-world fuel use varies with traffic, weather, air conditioning, and driving style.

Does this site track my location or data?

No. The calculators run entirely in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere. We do not ask for your location — use any map or price app you like to find stations near you.
